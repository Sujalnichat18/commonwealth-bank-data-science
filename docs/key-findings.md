# Selected social-media findings

These figures describe the 60-post practice dataset used in the exercise. They are not representative claims about the bank's live social-media channels.

| Topic | Posts |
|---|---:|
| Digital banking | 13 |
| Fraud & scams | 11 |

| Security education | 5 |
| Outage/service issue | 5 |

## Engagement by topic

| Topic | Total engagements |
|---|---:|
| Fraud & scams | 36,283 |
| Financial wellbeing | 23,309 |

| Digital banking | 21,176 |

## Sentiment and response needs

- Positive: 25 posts
- Neutral: 20 posts
- Negative: 15 posts
- Marked as requiring a response: 11 posts
- Not marked as requiring a response: 49 posts

## Average engagement rate by content format

| Content format | Posts | Average engagement rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Text + video | 4 | 5.13% |
| Text + image | 14 | 4.37% |
| Link | 6 | 3.69% |
| Text | 36 | 1.72% |

## Interpretation

A possible business follow-up would be to prioritise posts flagged as requiring a response, review recurring fraud and security questions, and test whether richer content formats perform consistently in a larger dataset. These observations are descriptive and do not establish causation.
